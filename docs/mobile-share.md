# Install Duet and share from Android

Use Chrome on Android and open the HTTPS address shown by Duet on your PC. Follow the [HTTPS and certificate trust guide](https.md) so the phone can verify the connection. Your phone and PC need to be on the same private network. If the PC's address or port changes, reopen the new address and install from there if needed.

## Install on your phone

1. Open the trusted HTTPS page in Chrome. Wait for Duet to load.
2. Open Chrome's menu and choose **Install app**, or **Add to Home screen** and then **Install** if Chrome offers both choices.
3. Find Duet in the app drawer and open it. Scan the QR code from the PC or enter its PIN to pair.

## Share files, text or a link

1. In an Android app such as Files, Photos or Chrome, select an item and tap **Share**. Choose **Duet** in the Android share sheet.
2. Review the draft in Duet. Enter the PC's PIN if prompted. Pairing leaves the draft waiting for your approval.
3. Tap **Send to PC**. Files appear in the transfer queue and text or links appear in **Shared text**. Use **Discard draft** to remove an unsent share from the phone.

Duet keeps unconfirmed drafts in the phone browser for up to one hour. Expired drafts are deleted the next time the browser runs Duet if the app was closed or suspended. An inbox can hold five drafts and 100 MiB of files total. Each share can include up to 20 files and 10,000 characters. Send or discard old drafts if the inbox fills up. For a larger file, open Duet and use **Choose files**.

After confirmation, an interrupted upload appears in the usual transfer queue. If the page was reloaded after confirmation, use **Resume** and select the original file.

## Copy a message

In **Shared text**, tap **Copy** on the message. Duet reports whether the phone copied it. If the browser refuses clipboard access, select the message and copy it manually.

## Phone checklist

- Open the trusted HTTPS address and install Duet through Chrome. Launch it from the app drawer.
- Share a generated file and a link from separate Android apps. Both should appear as local drafts, including after a reload.
- Pair with the displayed PIN and confirm that neither draft reaches the PC until you tap **Send to PC**.
- Send one draft and discard the other. Reopen Duet and confirm both drafts are gone. Verify only the sent content reached the PC.
- Tap **Copy** on a shared message and paste it into a different app. Switch Duet to Arabic and check the draft and clipboard labels.
